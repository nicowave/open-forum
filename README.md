# Open Forum

This is where readers talk back about the [Open Forum](https://nico-tek.com/open-forum) pieces on nico-tek.com.

Build notes tell the story of one thing I built: the problem, the choices and the limits. Research notes look at a question with public data, and every figure is dated and sourced. How-to notes teach a method I use, step by step.

## Pieces

- [How I built DMV Signal](https://nico-tek.com/open-forum/dmv-signal): a live map of permits, zoning cases and property sales across six DC-area jurisdictions. [Discuss it](https://github.com/nicowave/open-forum/discussions/1).
- [Do data centres set your power and water prices?](https://nico-tek.com/open-forum/data-centres): research notes on three regions, using public data only. [Discuss it](https://github.com/nicowave/open-forum/discussions/2).
- [Who really visited? Telling people from test traffic in Google Analytics](https://nico-tek.com/open-forum/real-visitors): how-to notes on four clues that tell visitors from machines, and the settings that keep the count honest. [Discuss it](https://github.com/nicowave/open-forum/discussions/3).

## Taking part

- Open the **Discussions** tab to ask a question, correct something or suggest a better way.
- Keep it about the work. No ads, and no personal details about anyone.
- I read everything. The good ideas change the next version.

No GitHub account? Use the email link at the end of each piece instead.
